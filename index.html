05 Scoring trend

Everything above adds up to this — your score to par, round by round, on control-chart footing. Below zero is the goal; lower is better, so a point below the lower limit is a breakthrough round and one above the upper limit is a blow-up. Whenever a signal fires the limits recalculate, so each stable stretch is judged against its own baseline.
