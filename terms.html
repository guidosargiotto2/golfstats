  • Course data can contain errors or be out of date. Check it against the club's scorecard, and tell us when something is wrong.

Statistics, strokes gained, predictions and control limits are estimates built from the data you provide; imported data (for example from a watch) can contain errors. They are information to help your decisions on the course — not swing instruction, medical advice or a guarantee of any score or handicap.
